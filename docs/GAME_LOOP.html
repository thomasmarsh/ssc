SSC · design direction · 2026-10-09

Build a home that lets you go farther.

Explore a reachable frontier. Gather, fight, trade, or complete work. Learn and equip a new capability. Bring the value home, improve its supply and defenses, and prepare the next expedition.

Target design / TODO Documentation only

Full rules, dependencies, and acceptance gates: GAME_LOOP.md. Built-to-target milestones: ROADMAP.md. Existing gameplay remains unchanged.

Two approaches, one expanding frontier

Warlike

Wildlife supplies raw materials, practice, and organs. Civilization raids supply equipment and a bounded archive. Conquest pays quickly and closes a source of future research.

Peaceful

Mining, farming, delivery, survey, and diplomacy pay for equipment and learning. Cooperation takes more work and grants deeper access to a civilization's specialties.

  1. Explore and earn: raw goods, jobs, trade, or raids.
  2. Learn and equip: knowledge, equipment grade, and realm counters.
  3. Grow home: factories, docks, fleets, and defenses supply the next expedition.

Essential progression has a peaceful route. Technology comes from civilizations or engineered sites; wild elders yield biological prizes.

Six primary resources

▰MetalRaw · builds and repairs
◌VolatilesRaw · chemical feedstock
◇CrystalRaw · sensors and tuning
✳BiomassRaw · crops and organ support
△FuelManufactured · buy or refine
≈WaterBulk utility · site tanks

All six have fixed HUD counters and one authoritative inventory model. Fuel and water use dedicated ship reserves. Large water deliveries belong to the supply network; processed goods and components stay in inventory and project previews.

Working defaults

Stock weapon and basic flight remain available without purchased fuel. Crops are the primary biomass source; carcass harvesting is deferred. Site stocks are local, reserved, or in transit, never a free galactic wallet.

Water makes the network useful

  1. Source pad: extract water into tanks and construct a tanker at the shipyard.
  2. Owned tanker: spend fuel and travel time carrying finite water cargo.
  3. Destination pad: unload into tanks to supply crops and industry.

Both endpoints must exist. The ship carries commissioning water, not routine bulk supply.

First connect two pads. Hubs, shared fleets, priorities, and interplanetary distribution follow a working route with explicit blockage and loss behavior.

Power keeps growing; combat stays readable

Equipment grade

Frontier sources and research provide stronger offense and durability. Drone, escort, turret, and ship equipment share this growth. A prepared player can push outward; a bare jump to (100,100) remains lethal.

Bounded combat shape

Keep limits on speed, slots, projectile count, reaction windows, resistance, and effect complexity. Realm counters add capabilities, with reachable peaceful sources outside the gated area.

Score, experience, and knowledge

Score and chains stay performance feedback. Combat, diplomacy, production, and exploration experience may support an optional rank, with benefits deferred. Knowledge is learned or captured; it is never bought with combat score.

Open decisions, with defaults

Barter first; currency remains open. Corpse harvesting and rank benefits wait for evidence they improve the loop. A proposed civilization capture allowance is one blueprint plus partial progress on a second; cooperation offers deeper access. Remote incidents use saved, bounded losses. Citizens need a separate purpose and design.

The early risks are losing gear sources before replacements exist, hidden combat gates for traders, production becoming waiting, and logistics duplicating or teleporting stock. Each milestone must address these before expanding.
