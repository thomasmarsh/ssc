## Reconciliation with FLOW

FLOW section 3 proposes three tabs together with automatic paid repair, automatic stash overflow, merged skills, and different material roles. This slice builds the three tabs and clearer descriptions only. Existing repair, stash, skill, economy, and organ rules take precedence over those proposals.

- The actual arsenal bench raises **already owned** profiles. It has no price or transaction for acquiring an unowned profile. WEAPONS shows the stock gun, owned profiles, and unowned profiles with the existing part/charge discovery requirement. It offers no new acquisition purchase. Stock has no paid levels; Tail Gun has a one-level cap.
- The actual rig contains 19 skills, not FLOW's earlier count of 16. All 19 identities, levels, prices, effects, and prerequisites remain separate. MINING has beam power/range, yield, magnet, and cargo; FLIGHT / UTILITY has parry, dash, beacon, shove, and plating; SONAR has all eight existing upgrades; ORGANS has symbiosis and all four existing strains.
- Repair remains an explicit instant action that independently buys as much missing hull and shield as the hold can pay. Ordinary landed mending and automatic field repair are unchanged. The preview shows the achievable hull/shield values and their actual costs, including partial repair.
- Reforge keeps the original best-of-three, never-worse roll and charges even when no better roll is found. A preview cannot promise a random result: it shows current effects and the rating floor. Rarity upgrade shows current effects, current/next rarity, and the guaranteed positive-stat multiplier; penalties remain, crossing into Rare raises traits, and a new affix is still rolled by the original transaction.
- The manual pad stash remains 100 per material, moving up to 25 per action, with existing partial transfers, hold-cap checks, and rejection behavior. It appears at the bottom of PARTS. There is no automatic overflow.
- Material colours and actual numeric costs remain. Metal, crystal, and volatiles retain every current role and cross-use. The view totals duplicate material price entries, including Homing's two crystal entries; the transaction still receives the original price.
- Organs retain first-graft costs, free subsequent fitting, oldest-slot replacement when full, loans, upkeep, dormant states, and retained ownership after removal. Removal stays available with no fuel or open slots. The selected graft says which fitted organ it replaces. Veil and Skip Node describe their DASH dependency without adding a new purchase gate.

## Navigation and state

Up/Down selects a row; Left/Right cycles PARTS, WEAPONS, and SKILLS, wrapping. Keys 1, 2, and 3 select tabs. Enter or Space confirms. E closes. Controller D-pad up/down or LB/RB selects rows, D-pad left/right selects tabs, A confirms, and B/Select closes. The mouse wheel moves the selected row. Existing row wrapping is preserved.

PARTS starts with repair, followed by paired REFORGE and UPGRADE rows for each fitted part, then one stash row per material. Each action names its part and slot. Enter on a stash row stores; Q or controller X takes. Q/X elsewhere does nothing. Adjacent part actions keep the existing navigation intact without adding an action mode or another binding.

Locked, unaffordable, maximum-level, and unowned entries remain selectable. A purchase never removes a row or moves selection. Buying a sonar tier leaves the same row at MAX LEVEL; grafting changes the same row to REMOVE; removal changes it back to GRAFT. The selected row is marked with `>` and repeated with its action, state, description, costs, and hold below the list. Green group headings and explicit states supplement colour. READY/UNLOCK is actionable, LOCKED shows the unmet prerequisite, UNAFFORDABLE shows the actual costs, and MAX LEVEL/MAX RARITY spends nothing. The original purchase ring, pickup sound cue, ability NEW tags, and starter guidance remain. Purchase receipts and fitted-part guidance are described below.

Input uses the existing just-pressed keyboard/controller edges. Simultaneous Enter, Space, and controller A dispatch once; confirmation wins over simultaneous Q/X so a stash press cannot store and immediately withdraw. Holding a button does not repeat a transaction. Separate presses still perform separate purchases.

## Purchase results and unlock guidance

FLOW section 7's purchase show is built as a concise receipt and the existing vector ring, rather than a new part-plugging animation. Each selected confirmation captures the fitted loadout, hull/shield, and cargo, dispatches the original transaction once, and then formats its actual outcome. The snapshot and formatting in `simulation/bench_feedback.rs` are read-only. They draw no RNG, predict no random affix, and perform no second purchase. Transaction rules remain in pads and organs.

- Repair reports actual hull and shield before/after, marks PARTIAL when damage remains, and lists the amounts really spent. Hull and shield retain independent payment.
- Rarity and reforge receipts identify the fitted part by slot, fitted-list number, and current name. Names longer than 28 characters shorten explicitly. They report old/new rarity and rating to three decimals, plus up to two changed part stat/trait totals and the number of further changes. The selected description gives current effects and the next-action preview. Unusually dense descriptions can shorten explicitly at small sizes, retaining their final requirement/preview lines. A reforge can trade one stat for another while increasing total rating. A paid unchanged reforge says `NO BETTER ROLL - KEPT` and shows payment. The preview remains the existing rating floor and best-of-three rule, with no promised result.
- Weapon purchases report the real old/new owned level and ammo cost per volley or launch. Skill receipts report the real old/new level and its relevant effect: rate, reach, yield, extra pull, hold, block chance, dash distance, sonar reach/speed/recharge/extra targets, reveals, beacon limit, ram push, own-impact share, or organ slots. These are existing getter values, not a second effect table. They describe the purchased track, with realm and situational modifiers continuing to apply in flight.
- Grafts report the strain's level, filled slot counts, and every replaced organ by name with `(kept)`. Removal reports freed slots and retained strain ownership. First-graft payment and free refitting come from actual cargo changes. Loans, upkeep, dormancy, and DASH-dependent effects are unchanged.
- Refusals retain the original reason and dry cue, replace the previous receipt immediately, and add no success ring or purchase cue. Maximum levels and rarity spend nothing. Stash transfers retain the original amounts and manual semantics, including sub-threshold rejection behavior.

There is one current receipt, lasting **four simulation seconds**, replaced on each success or refusal. The bench displays it independently of world notices, so a later unrelated notification cannot steal it. The same receipt appears once in the existing external feed after closing; repeated purchases remove the previous receipt from that feed. It retains the original fade there. The bench uses steady rarity colour for success and red for refusal, and reserves the complete wrapped result before budgeting list rows. Purchase rings retain their existing **0.7 real-second** life, but a new purchase replaces the active purchase ring, including several purchases within one frame. The existing **0.24-second shared pickup chime** and dry cue keep mixer coalescing, rate limits, voice limits, and mute behavior. Inspection found that the mixer does not implement FLOW's proposed rarity-specific pitch. Reduce effects removes purchase rings and retains steady result text and sound. No shake, blocking ceremony, or navigation delay is added.

On the first fitted Rare-or-better Plating, Engine, or Core that satisfies an unowned PARRY, DASH, or SYMBIOSIS requirement, guidance says **purchase available at the bench (SKILLS). Materials still required.** It is emitted after actual fitting or a rarity crossing, not for a scrapped pickup. Affordability does not suppress it. Already-owned tracks are skipped, and repeated qualifying parts do not repeat it. Up to three pending names share one **eight simulation-second** message. Buying a named skill or losing its fitted prerequisite removes that pending name; refitting later does not reannounce. Restart clears the run's announcement flags. The guidance neither buys a skill nor sets its ability NEW tag. NEW remains the original bought-but-unused ability state. The starter chain and discovery lifecycle are untouched.

## Layout and bounds

The panel is at most 680 logical pixels wide, inset 16 pixels from the right, and uses the existing UI scale. Its height is budgeted between the top HUD and bottom controls. Compact action rows are centred around selection; group headings count against the budget. The visible range and total row count make scrolling explicit. Long names shorten in the list and selected action heading; descriptions wrap at the available width. When a dense description and receipt compete for space, the description marks itself as shortened, retaining its final two requirement/preview lines. The complete receipt, guidance, costs, selected action, and controls keep their room. State, cost, controls, the current receipt, and pending unlock guidance have reserved space. The external notice feed gives way to the open menu to prevent overlap. Numeric material costs and hold amounts use the existing metal, crystal, and volatile colours. The SKILLS list has 23 selectable rows, with no separate organs tab.

The supported small capture for this slice is 800x600. Smaller windows are not a new support target. The bench can cover much of the world at this size because it is a landed menu. Human testing must still judge readability on a physical display and controller comfort.

## Validation and bounded galleries

The added scenarios cover all three tabs and four groups, wrapping boundaries, every skill and weapon transaction mapping, part index mapping, RNG-free views and rejected purchases, partial repair, rarity/reforge, unlock gates, affordability, caps, graft/removal/replacement, free refitting, stash access, and stable selection. Desktop tests exercise keyboard/controller bindings, simultaneous confirmations, held inputs, stash confirmation versus alternate input, and selected details/costs/controls at both layout budgets.

```sh
SSC_OFFSCREEN=1 SSC_OFFSCREEN_SIZE=1280x800 SSC_BENCH_VIEW=parts SSC_SMOKE_FRAMES=120 SSC_SCREENSHOT=/tmp/ssc-bench-parts.png cargo run --bin ssc
```

Modes: `parts`, `upgrade`, `weapons`, `skills`, `gate`, `organs`, and `stash`. The gallery stages existing progress, lands at the HOME pad, opens the real bench, and holds the pose. It only runs with the bounded smoke runner. `parts` includes multiple fitted actions and an unaffordable rarity purchase; `weapons` includes owned, purchasable levels, unowned, and maximum entries; `skills` selects LODE ECHO with sealed-organ discovery; `gate` selects parry's prerequisite; `organs` scrolls to the last owned strain with a full slot; `stash` selects crystal storage. Use `SSC_OFFSCREEN_SIZE=800x600` for the smaller capture. Metal on this host needs an unsandboxed run to access the GPU.

For receipts, add `SSC_BENCH_RESULT=1` to the bounded gallery command. It confirms the real selected transaction 20 frames before capture. Additional modes are `reforge-good`, `reforge-kept`, `unlock`, and `repeated`. The reforge poses use authored Rare parts with ordinary rolling code: a bare affix set guarantees improvement; an intentionally strong retained affix guarantees no better roll. `unlock` crosses Uncommon Plating into Rare; `repeated` confirms BEAM POWER three times in one frame with sufficient existing materials. Existing `upgrade`, `weapons`, `skills`, `gate`, `parts`, and `organs` cover upgrade, weapon/sonar purchases, locked/unaffordable refusal, and replacement. These hooks are available only within the bounded smoke runner. Use reduce effects and 800x600 as above.

Thirteen new library scenarios cover receipts against actual transaction state and original loot draws, partial repair, paid unchanged reforge, rejected and maximum purchases, weapon/skill effects, replacement/removal/free refitting, repeated receipts/payment/events, expiration, first fitted gates, already-owned skills, scrapped parts, loss/refitting, and restart. Five new desktop tests cover receipt/guidance/refusal layout bounds, dense-part description shortening, ring replacement and expiry, same-frame purchase coalescing, and reduce effects preserving text/audio. Existing input tests also check held confirmations do not alter receipt, payment, or cues. The existing Rare-plating installation test now checks the additional guidance and continued locked ability.

Generator version stays **19**: generated content, map compatibility, spawn indices, RNG draw counts, HOME, population thresholds, and the [discovery contract](DISCOVERY.md) do not change. Combat, Rift transit, mines, Weaver webs, Slinger orbits, and Rune sigils are unchanged.
