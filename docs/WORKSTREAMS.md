# Workstreams

The big areas of work, first recorded 2026-10-08 and revised 2026-10-09 for [GAME_LOOP.md](GAME_LOOP.md). That document owns the target loop and acceptance gates; this file maps systems and existing work to it. Each workstream has a goal, what exists today, design notes, slices in a sensible order, dependencies and open questions. Status goes at the end of each as slices land (tag unbuilt items `TODO:`). `SEED.md` holds the ordered queue; this file holds the thinking.

Shared principles (from `CLAUDE.md` and the brief):
- Fluid and natural first. Rich detail is welcome only where it can be ignored without friction: a player who never reads a tooltip should still get a good game.
- Opt-in depth. Buy or commission goods instead of owning every production chain. Peaceful work can earn essential technology and realm access. Wildlife, including apex elders, supplies raw materials and biological specials; technological conquest targets civilizations or engineered ruins.
- Expressions of genomes and seeds, not enum branches. New generators use salted streams; any generation change bumps the generator version and keeps the HOME golden unless changed on purpose.
- Rendering never owns rules. Simulation stays headless and deterministic.
- Performance: only sectors near the player simulate; every new population gets a hard cap and a test of that cap.

## Foundation: stochastic L-system infrastructure (feeds 1, 6, 7, 11, 3)

Status: slices (a) and (b) built for plants, and slice (d) reworked: creatures use a separate animal body-plan library (`src/anatomy.rs`, archetypes at depth 0 to 3, typed nodes, an optional `anatomy` genome section, `src/bodyplan.rs` expression, 11 specimens, `SSC_BESTIARY` gallery); design, the `Plan` type, templates, salting, caps and the shape-grammar recommendation are in [PROCGEN.md](PROCGEN.md). `TODO:` slice (c), wire into plants with workstream 1; juvenile growth stages for animal bodies (a juvenile is a single bead today).

Goal: one small, deterministic, tested module that grows branching and segmented structure from a seed, used for plants (1), creature bodies including apex elders (6) and hosted or brood bodies (7).
- Today: creature bodies are jointed chains driven by a flat gene list (`src/genome.rs`, `genome!` macro: real, int, categorical and trait genes). There is no grammar and no branching. Rooted life exists (`src/simulation/root.rs`) and plankton, but plants are not structured.
- Design: a stochastic parametric L-system with weighted rules whose weights and parameters are genes (so mutation and crossover act on the grammar), a turtle interpreter that outputs a tree of segments (length, angle, radius, kind), and a cap on symbols and segments. Output is plain data; the simulation turns it into joints or growth stages and the presentation draws it with gizmos. Growth is staged: a plant holds a seed, a derivation depth and a growth time, and renders the partial derivation, so growth is smooth and cheap.
- Slices: (a) module plus property tests (determinism, caps, mutation closeness), no gameplay; (b) a debug view (`SSC_SPECIMEN`-style hook and the dev panel) to look at samples; (c) wire into plants (workstream 1) first because it carries the least risk.
- Open: how much of the existing chain body generator is replaced versus left alone. Default is to leave the wild classics alone and use the L-system for new bodies only.

## 1. Farming

Goal: plant, tend and harvest crops with the mining beam, as a fluid loop that adds a new renewable resource.
- Today: mining beam and tough rocks (`src/simulation/mining.rs`), regrowing planetoids (`regrow.rs`), plankton and rooted life, cargo materials in `Cargo`.
- Target economy: BIOMASS becomes a primary raw resource in shared inventory and the HUD; planet crops sustain a closed cycle without explicit upkeep, and bioprocessing feeds nutrients, organ upkeep, and later fuel. Crop systems are already built; shared inventory and water are GAME_LOOP slices A and D. Historical planting sketch: Seeds are a pickup dropped by harvested wild plants and some creatures. Planting: fly slowly over a planetoid or a rock with soil, one key (use the interact button, contextual) drops a seed that anchors and grows by the L-system staged growth. Harvest with the beam: the plant yields by growth stage, ripe plants pay more and regrow from a stump; over-harvest kills the plant. Detail that can be ignored: crop varieties by seed genome (yield, growth time, hardiness, color) so keen players can breed, casual players just plant and reap. Pests and grazers are free ecology: wildlife eats unguarded crops, which gives farming a reason to defend.
- Slices: (1) crop plant entity with staged growth, harvest by beam, new material and HUD bar; (2) seeds, planting by interact, soil on planetoids; (3) crop genome and breeding via crossover of adjacent plants; (4) grazers and pests; (5) civilizations that farm and trade biomass.
- Depends on: L-system foundation (a)-(c). Controls budget (workstream 10): planting reuses interact, no new key.
- Status: slices 1, 2 and the edibility model are built (see [UNIVERSE.md](UNIVERSE.md) "Flora and farming"): species with chemistry, ship and creature palates, plants with clock-based growth on planetoids, planting by the interact key, beam harvest (ripe, unripe; Minecraft-style luck: a cut sometimes pays food and sometimes seeds, 1.05 seeds expected per ripe cut), seeds, biomass as a hull mend, grazing by palate, saved as a delta. Design change from the sketch: edibility is per species and per consumer (some crops are only for us, some only feed creatures), not a single crop flag. Seed pickups from creatures (a grazer's gut, 12 percent) and a seed picker (C, d-pad up) are built. Crop genome and breeding (slice 3) are built: four genes (yield, vigor, hardiness, hue) carried by plants and seed stacks, crossed with an adjacent mature plant of the same species when a ripe cut pays seeds (see UNIVERSE.md). Pests beyond grazing (slice 4) are built as blight (see UNIVERSE.md): planted crops outside HOME's sector fall ill, it spreads between same-species neighbors and drains growth, pruned by the beam, resisted by the hardy gene. `TODO:` shooting-based defense and a visible pest creature (blight is a plant state today, grazers do not carry it), blight on wild plants (left immune so forage never collapses), crop-gene HUD for planted crops (inspect a plant), a gene trade-off if breeding proves too easy. Shared ship biomass is built in slice A. Slice 5 and the greenhouse are built (see UNIVERSE.md "Flora and farming"): a gene-expressed `Territory::tillage` decides which civilizations farm (about a third; settlers always), they stock tended fields on planetoids and tended plots under glass at their seats, harvest into a granary, and a friendly farm pays a tithe back in biomass and bred seeds; cutting their crops costs regard. `TODO:` civilizations that replant, defend or plunder fields beyond regard, a granary shown at the seat, machines that use biomass (workstream 2).
- Decided (Thomas): crops grow only on planetoids. Plant-supporting planets sustain crops in a closed cycle without explicit maintenance or irrigation. Stations can grow them only inside an enclosed greenhouse-style module, never on bare hulls in open space.

## 4. Fast travel

Goal: reach new realms without long flights.
- Observation (Thomas): flying along a sector boundary, the ship passed almost unimpeded for a long way with very few collisions. `TODO:` investigate whether generation under-populates sector edges (placement margins) and fix, since it makes boundary corridors an unintended highway.
- Design: three layers. Hyperlanes: charted lanes between points of interest that the ship enters at a lane mouth, travels fast with the world streaming past as visual streaks, and drops out of at the end or on demand (the ship exits at normal speed and sees things as it flies by; hostile interdiction can pull it out early). Pad teleporters: a built pad can be upgraded to support teleport at high cost, both ends must be built; upgrades make a pad hard to spot (stealth) and pads can be installed at friendly civilizations. Civilization teleport service: non-hostile civilizations will teleport the ship to a destination for a high fee, scaled by distance and regard. Prefer hyperlanes to teleport where it makes the world feel connected, keep teleport for pad-to-pad and paid service.
- Today: Rift transit and beacons and star map jumps exist (`src/simulation/chart.rs`, `rift.rs`: personal rifts and seam needle are unbuilt). The jump to a beacon is the closest present mechanic.
- Slices: (1) edge-density investigation and fix; (2) hyperlane travel state in the simulation (stream sectors along the lane, cap speed by what must load, drop-out) with a deterministic test; (3) hyperlane rendering (streaks, parallax, passing landmarks); (4) pad teleport upgrade, both ends, stealth, friendly-civilization pads; (5) paid civilization teleport; (6) lanes to megastructures and realm borders.
- Depends on: sector streaming budgets (travel speed must not outrun loading). Controls (10): one contextual key for enter lane or confirm teleport.

Ore-rich fields also help mining and the Slinger's ore-rock complaint.
- Slices: (1) the care rule and tests (small, independent); (2) asteroid-rich field generator with its own salt and a population test; (3) range and biome retuning so weavers and slingers concentrate there.
- Depends on: feelings (9) for the field generator.

## 7. Creatures inside creatures

Goal: megafauna with symbiotic or parasitic residents, and large broods.
- Today: parasites and attached hostiles (`parasite.rs`), Hullworm and Remora symbiosis on the ship, passengers on rift transit, eggs and nests.
- Design: a host genome gets hosted-slot genes (count, kind, relationship: symbiote, parasite, brood). Residents spawn attached, ride the host, and are released on death or damage thresholds (a death releases a brood). Relationship affects behavior: symbiotes defend or clean the host, parasites drain it, broods swarm. Use the same attach mechanics as Hullworm so the code is shared. Display: residents visible on the body.
- Slices: (1) host slots and attached residents on one megafauna; (2) release rules and brood swarm; (3) symbiote and parasite behaviors; (4) ecology (species pairings by niche).
- Depends on: swarm budgets (8).
- Status: slice 1 built (2026-10-08). Shared attach mechanic in `src/simulation/attach.rs` (seat on the host frame, clear-arc search, capacity; used by rooted life, the Hullworm latch and residents). `Genome::hosted` is an optional nested section (`src/hosted.rs`: count up to `MAX_RESIDENTS` = 6 and a relation, symbiote, parasite or brood); the apex elder is the one host (rare, big, deterministic, already last in its sector). A quarter of lesser and half of major elders carry two to four residents (`apex::HOSTED_SHARE`), spawned rooted to the elder's head (`Rooting` with a creature host), counted against `SECTOR_BODY_BUDGET`, derived by `hosted::resident` (one body, no brain, the host's colours). They ride the head like rooters ride a rock, never collide with it, and are released dazed the tick the host is found missing (dead, eaten or unloaded); free creatures never take hold of a carrier. Generator version 21 (HOME unchanged). Display: residents are small bodies on the head with a collar, no roots.
- Slice 2 (partly built, 2026-10-08): a brood resident (`hosted::is_brood`, read from its genes) whose host is lost is released provoked for `root::SWARM_RAGE` seconds instead of dazed, so the orphaned young swarm the ship; symbiotes and parasites still scatter dazed. Residents of a slain elder are skipped when its sector reloads (`populate`). Damage-threshold release is built (`root::shed_brood`: a host keeps at most `ceil(count x health fraction)` brood, so each threshold crossed frees one, swarming; stateless so reloads settle the same). Slice 2 is done. Slice 3 built (2026-10-08): `root::tend_hosts`, read from genes via `hosted::is_symbiote` and `is_parasite`: each symbiote heals its host (`TEND`), each parasite drains it and feeds (`DRAIN`, floored at `DRAIN_FLOOR` of its health so parasites never kill). Active symbiote defense is `TODO:`. Slice 4a built (2026-10-08, generator version 22): the relation is picked by niche, `hosted::niche_weights` from the host country's biome character (calm lands favour symbiotes, savage and strange lands parasites, schooling lands broods; every pairing stays possible). Slice 4b built (2026-10-08, generator version 23): residents beyond the head. `Root::socket` (and `Rooting::socket` at spawn) names a `Socket` mark of the host body (an index into its marks, `BodyPlan::socket_slots`); `apex::seed_residents` seats the first residents on the sockets (one each, in slot order) and any beyond the socket count ride the head as before, so a body with no sockets is unchanged; `root::sync_roots` places a socketed resident from `Game::socket_pose` (the mark placed like `chain_decorations`, with the carrying bead's velocity) every tick, and a resident whose socket or bead is gone falls back to its head anchor. Release, shedding, healing and draining read the head id and are unchanged. Slice 4c built (2026-10-08, generator version 24): species pairing by range. `hosted::partner` draws a neighbour from the host sector's gene pool (never the host's lineage; smaller and commoner species likelier), and a symbiote or parasite wears that species' colours and outline (`hosted::resident_of`); a brood stays the host's own young, and a host alone in its pool keeps the old tint. Gameplay genes are untouched, so the relation is still read from genes. Workstream 7 is done apart from active symbiote defense (`TODO:`).

## 8. Swarms and rallied forces

Goal: bigger flocks in general, and hostile civilizations that rally forces so high alert is a real danger beyond the local screen.
- Today: small sparse groups, bogey schooling, civilization alert levels and territory (`src/simulation/diplomacy.rs`, `civ.rs`).
- Design: alert is a civilization-level value that, when high, dispatches rally forces that pursue the ship across sectors with escalating waves, are visible on sonar and the map as incoming marks, and ratchet down when the ship leaves territory or pays. Flocks: bigger schools and herds by genome (instanced cheaply: a flock is one logical entity with member state in a flat array, lighter collision, level-of-detail by distance).
- Slices: (1) flock data structure and one big herd with a budget test; (2) rally dispatch with caps (global and per civilization) and tests; (3) sonar and map markers; (4) de-escalation rules; (5) tune against the playtest.
- Depends on: performance caps. Open: global cap and what happens on very low-end machines.
- Status: slice 1 built (2026-10-08, generator version 28, HOME golden untouched). `src/simulation/flock.rs`: a `Flock` is one entity with members in a flat `Vec<Member>` (position, velocity, wing phase, health), never `Body` values, so the 1500-body cap is untouched. Boid steering (separation, alignment, cohesion, a pull to the flock's own centroid, a calm drift heading) from the genome: `flocking` is perception and cohesion, `cruise` and `speed` the calm and chase pace, `radius`, `hull` (members have 40 percent), `sight`/`lose`/`trigger` when it takes notice (a passive school stays calm until approached or hurt, hysteresis as for lone creatures; `Harm` ignores distance), `contact_damage` the sting (half a sting a second per touching member, at most 5). Light collision: members bounce off the ship and off at most 24 nearby rocks and wells as circles, never off each other or creatures. LOD by distance from the ship to the flock's edge: Near under 1800 steps every tick, Mid under 3400 every 4th tick with the time it missed, Far moves as one centroid and flushes the drift into the members every 16 ticks. Caps: 320 per flock, 640 loaded. Cost is O(n^2) pair tests per full step (300 members: 89,700, measured about 0.5 ms for a whole game tick at Near in an optimised debug build); a spatial grid is the upgrade path. Shots, blasts (`explode` and bullet bursts) kill members (one shot kills one; pays a tenth of the bounty); a flock shot to nothing is recorded in `Game::fallen` under `flock::CLEARED`. Nothing is saved: `herd::plan(seed, sector)` (`src/herd.rs`, its own salted stream, ring 3 and out only, the sector's most abundant passive school, 100 to 300 members, `HERD_CHANCE` 0.2) regenerates the herd on sector load. Drawn by `src/flockview.rs`. Not yet: radar, sonar and map marks (slice 3), loot, ram and mine damage to members, stealth reducing notice, per-flock persistence of thinned herds.

## 9. Feelings: urban, wild, desert

Goal: three distinct moods: urban (civilizations and megastructures), wild (low-tech grazing, predators, mostly unaware of the ship), desert (empty expanses and asteroid belts). They are flavors, not biomes: a lens laid over biomes and realms.
- Design: a per-sector feeling value derived from existing fields (civilization territory and its tier give urban, creature abundance gives wild, low life with high or zero rock density gives desert), used by generation (density, rock fields, ruins) and presentation (palette, sound bed, pacing: wild is quiet and peaceful, desert is vast and sparse with rare big finds, urban is busy). Lean into each: wild sectors favor unaware herds, desert sectors have long travel and a payoff, urban sectors have traffic.
- Slices: (1) a pure `feeling(seed, sector)` view-model with tests (no generation change); (2) presentation hooks (palette and ambient sound per feeling); (3) generation tilts, behind a generator version bump; (4) desert rock-field generator (shared with 5).
- Depends on: nothing; unblocks 3, 5, 8.

## 11. Builder creatures

Goal: creatures that perform simple construction, expanding what weavers already do (gathering asteroids and stringing webs). Added 2026-10-08; design not started.
- Direction: builders are genome expressions like everything else (a build-behavior gene set: what material they gather, what structure grammar they follow, how patient, how territorial). They gather rocks, ice or biomass and assemble nests, walls, bridges, hive structures, lures or trap fields using the same grammar infrastructure as plants and megastructures (the L-system or structure grammar produces the plan, the builder realizes it over time). Structures are persistent world state (see 12), can be mined, raided or repaired, and give the ecology new niches and the player new things to read and exploit.
- Ties to: weaver and slinger rock care (5), asteroid-rich habitats (5, 9), the L-system foundation, megastructures (living ones may be built by creatures), and machines (builders could be allies or competitors).
- Status: slices (1), (2) and (3) built (see below). `src/structure.rs` is the shared `StructurePlan` (`from_plan`: one block `Site` per `Stem`, scaled and turned, cut to `MAX_SITES` = 48, parents always earlier); `src/builder.rs` is the optional genome section `Genome::builder: Option<Builder>` (material stone, ice or ore; grammar genes; `scale`; `blocks`; `patience`), inherited whole, absent in juveniles, never drawn by `sample`, so no wild creature has one and generation and the HOME golden are untouched (generator version stays 24). `src/simulation/build.rs` is the behavior: a builder with the section gets a `Work` (blueprint from `Domain::Structure` stream keyed by its spawn identity, origin where it first stood, a cursor) and places one pinned rock block per work interval (`Builder::work`: patience times 0.5 to 1.5 by stem length), strictly in index order, only when within `build::REACH` (260) of the site and the site is clear; at most `build::MAX_WORKS` (6) structures at once. Dev spawn and `SSC_SPECIMEN` name: `builder`. `TODO:` it does not yet gather; structures are saved and survive unload (12, `docs/PERSISTENCE.md`); blocks are plain pinned rocks, not yet repairable or raided; territorial and gathering genes.
- Slice (2) built (generator version 25): `Genome::nest_builder` (`src/builder.rs`) turns a share of power-free sampled species (`builder::SPECIES_SHARE` 0.18 of species, about 7 percent of sectors from ring 5, `SPECIES_RING` 4) into nest builders inside `range::Distribution::founder`, chosen by a salted hash so no sampling draw moves; they are calm grazers (unarmed, no fling, no learning, free-roaming, `Trigger::Harm`) that keep the species' own look, and live in niches like any species. `wild_min_ring` gives them ring 4. A builder with a structure in progress is leashed to its site (`build::home_pull`, beyond `LEASH` 130, steering in `steer_creatures`), a finished or given-up work (`STALL` 90 s blocked) is `done`, no longer counts against `MAX_WORKS` and frees the builder. `TODO:` gathering rocks as a visible step, no repair or raiding.

The gene set is the territory's style (`build::civ_style`: `Builder::from_hash` of the territory id, patience at most 5 s, at least 14 blocks), the nth structure's blueprint stream is keyed by territory and n, so it is the same whoever lays it. A structure starts only where the whole plan clears fixed bodies (`plan_blocked`: fortress walls, rocks, planetoids), at the worker's feet. All builders now walk their structure: the leash target is the next site (`builder_homes`), which fixed half-built abandoned structures (plans reach farther than `REACH`); creatures no longer count as occupying a site (they are pushed clear). Blocks of a civilization wear its tint (`civ_tint`, `civ_block_tint`). Measured near a horde capital: a first structure of 30 blocks completes in under 400 s, the second is under way by then. `TODO:` gathering rocks as a visible step (a builder fetching and spending a free rock instead of conjuring the block), civilization structures with purpose (walls, lures), saving (12).

## 12. Persistence and save to disk

Goal: save and load the run and the player-shaped world. Decided (Thomas): wanted, and multiplayer is wanted eventually but is far out of scope. Design now so it stays possible later.
- Principles: generation stays a pure function of the master seed and sector, so only deltas from generation are saved (dead creatures, taken loot, planted crops, built pads and machines, creature-built structures, chart, pins, beacons, regard, doctrine, run stats, player ship and loadout). One serializable `SaveState` struct holds everything player-created or divergent; version it. Before 1.0, default additive fields or refuse incompatible older layouts after a version bump; no migration code or fixtures. Simulation stays deterministic and headless with explicit state, which is also the base multiplayer would need (authoritative sim, snapshot plus input stream). No rendering state in saves.
- Slices: (1) inventory every piece of mutable state and classify it (derived, delta, ephemeral) in a doc; (2) `SaveState` with serialize and a round-trip test that a saved and reloaded game steps identically; (3) disk format, location and autosave, versioned; (4) UI: continue, new run, delete; (5) explicit format/generator compatibility checks, without old-format migration fixtures or code before 1.0.
- Status: slices 1, 2 and 3 built (inventory in [PERSISTENCE.md](PERSISTENCE.md); `SaveState` RON with version and generator headers, tested round trip, atomic file with backup, autosave). Slice 4 built (title menu continue / new / delete, saving on by default, a lost ship is saved at once; lives revive locally and exhaustion returns to the last visited pad or HOME with one life and the same progress). Builder structures are saved as deltas (kept per structure, restored block by block on sector load) and slain residents are pinned by a test. Migration fixtures are cancelled until 1.0.
- Must come before farming, machines and builders, which all create persistent state. Decided: retained legacy and wreck data stay in the run file; ordinary death creates no successor or bequest. NEW GAME clears progress and saved history.

## Order of operations (revised 2026-10-09)

The authoritative dependency/acceptance table is [GAME_LOOP.md](GAME_LOOP.md) section 13. [SEED.md](../SEED.md) holds the active queue. The earlier fixed generation batch, 19 -> 20 version instruction, and completed foundation phases are superseded; use the actual generator version and independent salted streams for each intentional generation change.

1. **First frontier:** A shared resources/HUD; B peaceful basic procurement plus honest loot; narrow C technology, bounded capture, and grade scaling. Land B's substitute sources with removal of wildlife gear. Confirm both peaceful and warlike paths to a harder frontier.
2. **Working home:** D local refinery/water/crops/production; narrow E delivery and research partnership. Require persistent transactions and useful recipes, not a complete market.
3. **Remote industry:** F mining drones and upgrades/losses; G pad-built tanker/two-pad bulk material route. Hard caps, finite ore, and cargo conservation precede multi-hop routing.
4. **Living trade and defense:** E2 independent diplomacy and commercial terms precede H visible merchant arrivals, real supply dependency, attributed privateering, sensors/turrets/escorts and bounded saved remote incidents.
5. **Network and districts:** I distribution hubs and player-grown megastructure. J citizens only after a separate design establishes their purpose.

Cross-cutting dependencies:
- Use one goods/price/transfer API for bench, farms, machines, markets, contracts, and fleets. Physical stock stays local or in transit; a UI preview cannot spend it twice.
- Keep knowledge, held equipment, grades, and experience separate. Tech-graph alternatives cover essential peaceful progression and realm counters.
- Extend SaveState in each slice. Define missing generated-anchor behavior before persistent factories/fleets depend on it; no save migrations before 1.0.
- Budget bodies, fleet records, jobs, routes, retained outcomes, and catch-up events. Unloaded operations are explicit bounded state transitions; closing the game grants no wall-clock production by default.
- Use a shared cross-sector plan query for generated layouts and lanes; player construction additionally needs mutable ownership and paid-progress deltas.
- Audit context/controller navigation as each panel lands. Trade, planting, bench, and site actions use existing context conventions.
- Register constants in DEVTOOLS B when available; tooling never blocks a feature. Human playtest after each milestone.

Backlog alongside this order: creature fill, rally forces, weaver care, density/feeling work, hyperlanes, generated megastructures, and polish. They remain open, but no longer define the economy's prerequisites unless a slice explicitly needs them.

Decisions retained from 2026-10-08: sector size is 6000; deterministic/headless simulation; disk saves wanted; multiplayer eventually but out of scope; crops on planetoids or enclosed greenhouse modules; one contextual interact is the starting assumption. HOME's golden changes only deliberately, not as an automatic part of a historical batch.
